The Cluster×Satellite×Field Bispectrum

A configuration-resolved estimator for galaxy-cluster assembly bias
The idea

Measure assembly bias from the full configuration of the three-tracer bispectrum. The opening angle φ between the intra-halo pair and the large-scale mode splits the signal into a density response (does richness track overdensity?) and a tidal response (do satellites align with the large-scale tidal field?).

The estimator — what to do

  1. Measure the angle-resolved 3-point cross-correlation ζ(s, r, φ) — keep the opening angle, don't collapse it.

  2. Decompose in multipoles of φ (equivalently the bispectrum in μ = k̂·q̂).

  3. ℓ=0 → b_occ: richness vs. overdensity. ℓ=2 → b_K: satellites vs. the large-scale tidal field.

  4. Subtract the mass bias b_c; validate with a shuffling null test in mocks.

The observable
Bcsf(k, q; φ) → [ bc + bocc + bK(cos²φ − ⅓) ] P(k)P(q) the opening angle splits it: ℓ=0 → bocc (density-type), ℓ=2 → bK (tidal-type). Either, non-zero at fixed mass, is assembly bias.
Deliverables

Estimator + Fisher forecast + a mock-validated measurement pipeline.

Data

DESI & Rubin/LSST galaxies with eROSITA, SPT-3G & redsequence clusters.

Key systematic

Projection mostly hits ℓ=0; the ℓ=2 tidal term & a shuffling null test are the controls.
