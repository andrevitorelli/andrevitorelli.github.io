MARC‑HI Mass-controlled Assembly-bias · Residual Clustering in HI
21 cm intensity mapping × spectroscopic groups · z ≈ 0.1–0.4 · ν ≈ 1015–1291 MHz · single-slide concept — for discussion

One b(M). Two tracers. The residual is assembly bias.

Mass-only occupation is the null: absent assembly bias, every tracer of a halo of mass Mh shares one large-scale bias b(Mh). HI is the sharpest secondary axis we have, so comparing an HI field and a galaxy field at fixed host mass isolates the secondary dependence directly in bias space.

  1. 01

    Bin by host halo mass

    Group spec-z galaxies; calibrate Mh by stacked g–g lensing. Split the 21 cm field by the same proxy via HI–optical cross-ID.

  2. 02

    Measure each bias per bin

    bg from Pgg; bHI from the foreground-immune cross Pg,HI/Pgg, at k ≲ 0.1 h Mpc−1.

  3. 03

    Test for a split

    One universal curve is the null; bHI(Mh) ≠ bg(Mh) ⇒ assembly bias. Cross-check with M(r) and r(k).

Data

MeerKLASS / SKA-MID single-dish 21 cm × DESI BGS + GAMA groups. Host masses from group catalogues and stacked g–g lensing (KiDS / DES / LSST) over ≈10³ deg².

Forecast signal

Expected |ΔbAB|/b ≈ 5–15% across 10¹²–10¹⁴ M⊙h⁻¹; roughly 4–6σ per dex. Blind nulls: r(k)→1 and a single b(M).

Payoff

First bias-space detection of gas-driven assembly bias at fixed halo mass; a direct prior on HOD secondary parameters and on 21 cm bias systematics for Stage-IV surveys.
