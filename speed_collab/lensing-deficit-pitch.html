Locating the lensing deficit with a second source plane at z ≈ 1100

Galaxy–galaxy lensing sits 20–40% below the amplitude implied by the same galaxies’ clustering (Leauthaud+ 2017). Lens the identical halos a second time with the CMB and the deficit stops being a mystery amplitude: the two measurements share one surface density ΔΣ(R), so their ratio is a null test that says what kind of problem “lensing is low” actually is.

The observable

ΔΣκCMB(R)  /  ΔΣγ(R)  =  Σcr,γ / Σcr,CMB  ≡  ℱgeo(zl)

Same lenses, same projected ΔΣ(R) → the mass profile, HOD, galaxy bias and baryonic feedback all cancel. In ΛCDM the ratio is pure cosmography — distances only. Any departure is diagnostic, not degenerate.
